Cursor Resize & Opacity

Hold Alt, press the right mouse button on the canvas and drag:

  • Left / right: brush size (minimum 1 px).
  • Up / down: painting opacity.

The first movement picks the axis; it stays locked until the right button is released. Small values are precise: 1–20 px take 8 screen pixels per brush pixel, bigger sizes grow faster. Opacity 1–20 % takes 5 px per percent, above that 2.5 px.

Turn it on or off in Tools > Scripts > Cursor Resize & Opacity. Tuning values are at the top of cursor_resize_opacity.py.
